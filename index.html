Our Story

Three generations, one plot of soil

Stackly Farm began in 1988 when Margaret Stackly turned four acres of tired pastureland into a kitchen garden that fed half the county. Today her grandchildren farm the same forty acres — still without chemical fertilizer, still rotating crops by hand-drawn maps she left behind.

We measure success in earthworm counts, not yield reports. A slower method, but it's the only one that leaves the land better than we found it.

How We Farm

From seed to your basket

Sown

Seed Saving

Every variety is open-pollinated and re-seeded from our own harvest each season.

Tended

No-Till Beds

Compost, cover crops and hand-weeding — never a synthetic input touches this soil.

Picked

Hand Harvested

Every crate is picked at peak ripeness the same morning it ships or sells.

Delivered

Direct to You

Farm stand, weekly boxes, or wholesale — no distributor sits between us and you.

Who We Grow With

Built on handshake agreements

Every co-op, mill and market stall we work with started the same way — a walk through the field and a handshake, not a contract. Thirty-eight years in, most of those partnerships are still standing on nothing more than that.

From the grain co-op that mills our wheat to the seed-savers who trade heirloom varieties with us each spring, Stackly Farm grows inside a small, trusted circle — and we're always looking to widen it the same honest way.
