What We Offer

Four ways to
taste the farm.

From weekly vegetable boxes to full farm-to-table events — here's how individuals, restaurants and communities work with Stackly Farm.

Service 01

Weekly CSA Boxes

A rotating box of whatever's ripest that week, packed the morning of pickup. Choose small, family or bulk sizes — pause or skip anytime.

  • Small, Family & Bulk box sizes
  • Pause, skip or swap weekly online
  • Farm-stand pickup or local delivery
Join the CSA →
Service 02

Wholesale for Restaurants

We currently supply six local kitchens with standing weekly orders. Menus built around what's actually in season, delivered twice a week.

  • Standing or seasonal wholesale orders
  • Twice-weekly delivery within 60 miles
  • Custom growing by request for chefs
Enquire Wholesale →
Service 03

Farm-to-Table Events

Long-table dinners, workshops and private gatherings held right in the orchard, catered entirely from what's growing that day.

  • Seated dinners for 10–80 guests
  • Seasonal menus, chef collaborations welcome
  • Private bookings & seasonal public dinners
Book an Event →
Service 04

Farm Tours & Workshops

Two-hour guided walks through the fields, greenhouse and apiary, plus hands-on seed-starting and composting workshops.

  • Weekend public tours, weekday private bookings
  • School and community group rates
  • Seasonal workshops: seed-starting, composting, beekeeping
Book a Tour →
How It Works

From sign-up to your doorstep

Getting real, seasonal food onto your table takes three simple steps.

STEP 01

Choose Your Plan

Pick a CSA box size, a wholesale account, or a one-off farm-stand order — whatever fits how you eat.

STEP 02

We Pack It Fresh

Everything is harvested and packed the same morning, based on what's genuinely at its peak that week.

STEP 03

Pick Up or Delivered

Grab it at the farm stand, a local drop point, or have it delivered within our 60-mile radius.
